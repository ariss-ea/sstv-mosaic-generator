SSTV MOSAIC DECOMPOSER

Extract SSTV frames and diploma from your mosaic

This tool splits a mosaic image created with the ARISS-EA SSTV Mosaic Generator into the original 6 or 12 SSTV frames (Robot36 or PD120, at their native resolution) and the diploma (if present). Download each image or the full set in one click.

How to Use

  1. Click Choose Mosaic Image and select your composite JPG.
  2. The tool automatically detects 6-image or 12-image mosaics, in Robot36 (320×240) or PD120 (640×496), with or without diploma. Mosaics made with older versions of the generator are supported too.
  3. Each tile shows its position as 1/N…N/N, or Diploma.
  4. Use the Download button on a tile to save it, or Download All to get a ZIP.
