How to Use

×
  1. Enter Name and Grid Square (Callsign optional).
  2. Click any No image slot to upload up to 12 SSTV frames (640×496 or matching aspect-ratio). Use the red Remove button to clear a slot.
  3. Upload the official Diploma (optional). Use the red Remove button to clear it.
  4. In the Background section, choose one of:
    • International Space Station
    • ARISS Antenna on Columbus Module
    • Pillars Of Creation (JWST)
    • High Contrast (White)
    • High Contrast (Black)
    • Upload your own (custom uploads get a semi-transparent overlay for text readability)
  5. Click Save Mosaic as JPG. The downloaded image includes:
    • ISS Expedition logo at top-left
    • Title and subtitle
    • Your personal info with high contrast
    • 4×3 image grid with slot numbers
    • Optional diploma on the right
    • Selected background
  6. Download and share your SSTV mosaic!
